About

Associate Professor (HDR) in Computer Science at the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science, UFR SEGMI, Université Paris Nanterre, UPL.

Associate member of the Laboratory of Analysis and Modeling of Systems for Decision Support (LAMSADE), UMR CNRS 7243, Université Paris Dauphine, PSL.

Research

Affiliation

Member of the "Data Science" team at the Laboratory of Analysis and Modeling of Systems for Decision Support (LAMSADE) at Université Paris Dauphine.

Research Axes

Evolutionary Algorithms (EA): Application to Machine Learning

  • EA for Time Series Regression - Application to Financial Market Volatility Prediction
  • EA for Massive Data Classification: Scaling Genetic Programming
  • EA for Clustering and Community Detection: Application in Gene Networks

Application of EAs for Solving Difficult Numerical Problems

  • EA for Model Calibration in Finance
  • EA for Difficult Combinatorial Optimization: Vehicle Routing Problems (VRP), Scheduling of Intensive Scientific Workflows (WS) in Cloud Environment

  • Hmida Hmida - "Extension of Genetic Programming for Supervised Learning from Very Large Databases (Big Data)" (2016-2019), co-supervised with Marta Rukoz (LAMSADE) and Amel Borgi (LIPAH - Tunis El Manar)
